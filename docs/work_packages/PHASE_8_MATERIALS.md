# PHASE 8 — MATERIALS / PRESENTATION

PREPARED ONLY; NOT STARTED. Apply the shared execution contract/tooling-readiness
table. Entry: stable Phase 7 body/garment hashes and bare/dressed evidence.

## Scope and exact work

Author self-contained numeric skin, clothing and presentation materials. Keep
athletic everyday anatomy legible at app distance with restrained highlights and
colour contrast. No third-party textures/HDRIs, image-derived likeness or detail
that conceals silhouette/contact defects. Geometry, skin weights, garment shape,
rig/rest, poses/handles, R2 and thresholds are frozen for this package.

Prepare a scene/material inventory before capture: owned material/node sources,
numeric parameters, object assignments, external-resource checks, lighting,
camera/projection, colour management, renderer/version and output dimensions.
Record intentional render differences rather than claiming matched comparisons.
Existing milestone capture is bare; dressed/app-distance capture needs its own
source-bound extension. Pose-render visibility is not application integration.

## Tests, renders and exit

Verify body/garment geometry and weights unchanged with raw audits; rerun bare and
dressed controls if presentation modifiers/visibility affect evaluation. Recheck
provenance and external-resource inventory. Materials cannot waive development,
production, garment or contact failures. Look for glare/shadow concealing joint
collapse, fingertips, waist clearance or floor penetration.

Produce matched bare/dressed neutral front/rear/side/3/4, app-distance shoulder
press/curl/squat/lunge/push-up/row and representative skin/cloth close-ups. Record
lighting/colour management/renderer state in source receipts. These are actual
candidate renders; later real-app views remain a Phase 10 requirement. Publish
NON-BLOCKING pending review and continue Phase 9/tooling evidence safely.

Exit checks: `owned_material_sources`, `stable_presentation_capture`,
`readable_application_views`, `no_concealed_body_failures`, `published_review_snapshot`.
The readability check records a real review and its viewing conditions; it is not
inferred from a render existing. Commit owned numeric material records, inventory,
unchanged-model audit, actual source-bound images/boards and verified exit/shared
state. Reject external sources, hidden failures, undocumented capture drift or
geometry/weight changes. Next: Phase 9 production deformation validation.
