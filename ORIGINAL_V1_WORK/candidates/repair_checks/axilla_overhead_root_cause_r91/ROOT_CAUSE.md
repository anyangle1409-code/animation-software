Method: numpy re-evaluation of the committed skinning dumps (exact LBS, verified against Blender to 4e-7 m) for r91, the weights-only mesh (r81 weights, no shape keys), r68 and r42 (= P3B1's candidate), in press_top and the arc entries; plus renders. ## What the three visible defects are

1. **Chest pit (front/lateral chest).** 53-60 left torso vertices, rest x -0.15..-0.06, y -0.13..-0.05, z 1.29..1.39 (upper lateral pectoral wall), weighted spine_02/spine_03 (clavicle 0.08). With weights only they sit on the thorax surface (offset to the trunk-rigid reference about 0). In r91 the abduction corrective moves them 4-7 cm INWARD along the surface normal (mean 4.2 cm, deepest 6.9 cm). The corrective produces dents only: no vertex is pushed outward more than 2 cm. In press_top it removes 1.23e-3 m^3, 1.5 percent of the mesh volume. The lower-medial rim of the pit is a new sharp edge: Laplacian/edge length 0.85 against 0.01 at rest and 0.01 weights-only (vertices 5967/5968).
2. **Shoulder-top knob and web (each side of the neck base).** Clavicle-weighted skin (0.7 clavicle, 0.2 upper arm), rest about (-0.14, -0.03, 1.53): 33 vertices move 2-3 cm, 90 percent tangentially, and the triangle area inside this set doubles (+95 percent) - the corrective stretches the skin to bridge the gap between the clavicle-driven and upper-arm-driven sheets. These knobs do NOT exist in the weights-only render, which is clean at the clavicle. Several previously smooth dihedrals there (cos 0.97) become creases (cos 0.2).
3. **Lateral/back wing flaps.** 60 vertices at rest (-0.16, y +0.06..+0.14, z 1.23..1.35), weighted spine_02 0.55 + scapula_l 0.45, sit 5-6.5 cm outside the thorax with weights alone (P3B1 / r42: 8.7 cm) and are moved only 0.5 cm by the corrective. This one is a skin-WEIGHT effect (scapula influence on lateral torso skin) that the corrective leaves alone because the drift allowance (a0 + a1*exp(-(r/rb)^2), up to about 0.28 m near the joint) lets it stay.

## Cause attribution

* Pit and knob/web: the abduction corrective. It is generic, not a press_top quirk: the dent grows smoothly with humerothoracic elevation (theta 62 deg 0.8 cm, 83 deg 2.6 cm, 104 deg 4.6 cm, 125 deg 6.3 cm, 145 deg 7.1 cm, 166 deg 6.9 cm) and the same 50-59 vertices are dented in press_top, press_top_rhythm, pullup_hang and pullup_hang_rhythm. At 82 degrees (arms horizontal) the shape is natural (pectoral edge, 2 cm dent): the defect only appears beyond about 100 degrees.
* Why the solver does it: the weights-only mesh has a steep arm-driven weight gradient across the chest/scapula transition (up to 0.27 per cm, p95 0.091 against 0.056 elsewhere) giving 38 over-stretched edges (ratio above 2.5) in press_top; the corrective's objective has stretch hinges and a p99 tail bound (--p99-tail 1.95, which exists to satisfy the development gate edge_ratio_p99 <= 2.0), barriers for folds/area/Laplacian and a trunk-anchoring term that only acts beyond the large allowance - and NO shape term for volume or inward displacement. Reducing the stretch tail (the 38 edges become 5, p95 of the pit-zone edges 2.56 -> 1.95) is cheapest by pulling chest skin toward the arm (median ratio of the pit-zone edges rises 1.18 -> 1.53) and by sliding clavicle skin to bridge the gap.
* Not the cause: topology/edge loops (no inverted or collapsed triangles in the zone: smallest triangle area ratio and dihedral extremes in the upper body are better than weights-only and far better than P3B1, upper-body hard folds 0 against 66 and 184), the skeleton, upper-arm axial rotation (identical dent in poses with 63 and 90 degrees), self-intersections (32 in press_top, below P3B1's 95), the flexion key (inactive).
* Why the metrics missed it: the comparator tracks per-region MINIMUM and MAXIMUM edge ratio, p01/p99, volume deviation (gate 0.9-1.1; press_top volume is 0.9945), self-intersections and counts of compressed/stretched edges. A 7 cm smooth dent changes none of them materially because edges inside it are only mildly compressed (region minimum 0.745) and the lost volume is 1.5 percent of the whole mesh. No metric measures departure of the surface from the underlying thorax.

## Ranked fixes by blast radius
1. Small refinement of the existing corrective (same mask, same driver, same weights): add a bounded-inward-displacement constraint (dent limit along the uncorrected normal) for torso skin, optionally with a volume floor (already available). No new keys, no weight, bone or baseline change. Full 15-pose evidence needed. RISK: the stretch tail the dent was absorbing may return (p99 gate 2.0, region maxima).
2. Tightly bounded new corrective keyed to genuine mechanics (e.g. a separate clavicle-top key): only if 1 fails.
3. Local weight smoothing of the chest/scapula transition (reduces the stretch at the source): non-skeletal, moderate risk (earlier smoothing moved squat/press/pull-up metrics).
4. Local topology/rig changes (helper bones, extra loops): not justified by this evidence.
