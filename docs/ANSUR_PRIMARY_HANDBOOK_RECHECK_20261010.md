Acromion uses a specified trapezius/clavicle construction on the lateral border.
Cervicale is a palpable spinous landmark, not a vertebral body centre; the
handbook explicitly distinguishes its convention from the earlier ANSUR one.
Radiale and stylion are palpable radial endpoints, not elbow/wrist centres.
Suprasternale refers to the inferior jugular-notch point.

The length measurement uses the right palm forward, whereas wrist height uses
palms medial and controlled upright posture. Inference: neither identical
posture nor a joint-centre substitute is justified by these definitions.
