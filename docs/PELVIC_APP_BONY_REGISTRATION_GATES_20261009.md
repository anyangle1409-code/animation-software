## Primary anatomy: what is APP?

The anterior pelvic plane (APP) is the coronal bony anatomical reference plane defined by **both osseous ASIS landmarks** and the midpoint of **both osseous pubic tubercles**. This reference should not be confused with the ISB's PSIS-based pelvic anatomical frame or automatically treated as a neutral standing world frame.

Sources:
- *Methods for three-dimensional characterization of the acetabulum prior to pelvic reorientation osteotomy: a scoping review* (2024), [PMC11370718](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11370718/), Fig. 2, exact anterior-superior iliac spine and pubic-tubercle semantics.
- *The Effect of Using Different Coordinate Systems on In-vivo Hip Angles Can Be Estimated from Computed Tomography Images* (2019), [PMC6800649](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6800649/), distinguishes **pAPP (ASIS + pubic tubercles)** from **pISB (ASIS + PSIS centre)**. These are not interchangeable.
- Imai et al. (2019) CT APP [PMC6506943](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6506943/), 55 men of mean stature 166.0 cm; sagittal S1-to-hip-centre mean DYp 18.8 ± 21.2 mm (2 SD), DZp 104.7 ± 20.5 mm (2 SD) and sagittal magnitude 107.0 ± 19.9 mm (2 SD). The values remain comparative context, NOT chosen HGPT bone-position targets.

## Delivered mechanical APP computation

`scripts/anatomy_fit/pelvic_app_bone_frame.py` defines a **right-handed** APP basis from seven 3D points (four for APP, plus S1 superior endplate centre and both femoral head centres):

1. **LEFT**: normalised right ASIS → left ASIS vector.
2. **SUPERIOR**: normalised midpoint of ASIS minus midpoint of pubic tubercles, projected orthogonal to LEFT.
3. **POSTERIOR**: cross(SUPERIOR, LEFT). Thus LEFT × POSTERIOR = SUPERIOR, in the canonical Home Gym PT x-left, y-posterior, z-up convention.

The APP origin for diagnostics is the ASIS midpoint. This is an **anatomical pelvic-bone frame**, not an assumption that the pelvis is upright in the world or aligned with the study scanner.

The code computes the 3D S1-to-hip-axis offset and projects it into posterior and superior APP components, with Euclidean sagittal magnitude. It detects degenerate ASIS separation, collinear pubic geometry, invalid coordinates, inverted/swap-labelled bilateral landmarks, and grossly incompatible world pose. Proper-frame handedness and global rigid transform invariance are unit tested.

### Separate anatomical-provenance gate

These geometrical results can be generated by **any** coordinate triplet and therefore **do not establish the actual bony points**. The provenance checks separately require for each of the seven bony landmarks:

- source-asset hash and documented extraction method;
- bone-segmented source geometry, not an r95 skin mesh, aesthetic surface landmark or arbitrary joint-stick anchor;
- explicit physical bony feature identity.

Even if all metadata is declared and geometry passes, **canonical approval remains false**: independent physical/clinical surface validation, cohort transfer and the study's exact endpoint-frame alignment must still be verified.

### Why c004 cannot yet establish APP

The old record's left/right `hip.asis_skin` comes from a surface vertex near an **aesthetic inguinal groove**. Its authors recorded **low confidence and ±20 mm uncertainty**. The `derived_points.pubic_symphysis_side` is a provisional pelvic control, not the right/left bony **pubic tubercle** surface locations. Using those four points to construct a numerical APP yields a **hypothetical frame only**.

`--legacy-record` intentionally emits `LEGACY_SKIN_FRAME_REJECTED`, even though it also computes illustrative APP axes and a neutral c004 S1-to-hip-axis offset. It runs eight perturbation cases (independently varying left/right ASIS Y/Z by ±20 mm) to quantify **geometric sensitivity**, without suggesting a probability distribution, a clinical error tolerance or a target shift. Those comparisons are not used for bone correction.

No values from Imai et al. may be applied as a displacement in the unregistered world-Y/Z frame, and the study male mean cannot be linearly rescaled from 166 to 182 cm.
