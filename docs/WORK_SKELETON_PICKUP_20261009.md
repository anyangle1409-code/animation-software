## Review sequence

1. Read `WORK_SKELETON_DEVELOPMENT_20261009.md`, the live tracker, policy and new evidence reports. Recheck primary source tables, endpoints, units, population and independent publication/cohort identity.
2. Run focused constructor/hand/queue tests and relevant regional tests. Run full discovery; retain exact failure names. Five failures/four errors inherited from older production orchestration remain unresolved. Do not change anatomy or weaken freeze logic to satisfy stale recovery expectations.
3. For each proposed coordinate, retain at least two compatible independent works, extraction locator, frame derivation, endpoint definition and review receipt. Verify bone lengths, axes, joint centres and participant connections; do not assume all bone tails are joint centres.
4. Validate the whole coupled skeleton through CP2. A numeric storage roundtrip PASS may coexist with anatomy FAIL. Reject missing contacts/envelopes and bad geometry rather than filling them from skin.
5. In Blender, use the retained diagnostic rehearsal for independent inspection of all regions, orthographic views, close-ups and movement extremes. Compare exact bone/marker identities, coordinates, source reference frames, carrier/parent fields and rest/motion hashes. Do not move parents to make reference and carrier names match.
6. After source-ready skeletal geometry exists, perform fresh construction/capture in separate processes, numerical comparison and visual/contact review. Repeat solver/Blender checks and appropriate coupled/load-bearing tests. Physiological observations, conditioning poses and arbitrary test excursions must remain distinguishable.
7. Only after all required anatomical gates pass may a new canonical candidate be proposed. Production skinning/character rebuilding waits. A future mesh must fit this independently accepted skeleton; negative clearance is a mesh defect diagnostic, not permission to relocate bones.
