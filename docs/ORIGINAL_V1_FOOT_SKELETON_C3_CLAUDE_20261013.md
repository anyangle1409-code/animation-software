## Heel position (the main uncertainty)

In a003 the ankle sits too close to the heel. The heel is 38 mm behind the ankle, while the ankle to the 1st MTP joint is 191 mm.

Evidence I could reach from this container:
- **Rajagopal2016 OpenSim model** (Delp-lineage foot; SHA matches the copy recorded in the repo). The posterior heel marker is 73.8 mm behind the ankle and the MTP axis is 130 mm in front, for a generic 1.70 m subject. Scaled to 1.82 m that is 79.0 and 139.2 mm. Details: `c3/rajagopal2016_foot_partition_crosscheck.json`. These are marker centres, and the model is a single lineage.
- **ANSUR II.** Once the heel is placed, ANSUR fixes the 1st MTP joint at heel − 207.4 mm and the toe tip at heel − 279.9 mm. With a 60 mm heel, the ankle-to-MTP1 distance is 147 mm, which agrees with the Rajagopal MTP axis (130–139 mm) within the obliquity of that axis.
- **Secondary ratio (not verified).** A foot-anthropometry ratio of pternion-to-lateral-malleolus ≈ 0.18 × foot length (about 50 mm) appeared only in a search snippet. The proxy blocked PMC, DTIC and the publishers, so I could not open the primary tables.

The heel distance is therefore set to **60 mm, a provisional central value**. The plausible band is **50–72 mm** and is exposed as `--heel-behind-ajc-mm`. A different value only moves the boundary between heel and midfoot; the ANSUR length and ball-of-foot targets are met either way.

## How C3 was built

All scripts are on this branch.
- `scripts/build_original_foot_skeleton_candidate_blender.py`:
  - **Bones.** Each ray uses a piecewise-linear map along the foot, applied identically to the 52 foot bones of the 206-bone master, the runtime `foot_*` tails and `toe_*` controls, and the 78 bone-parented joint and landmark empties. The ankle-joint centre stays fixed; the midfoot between the ankle and the TMT joints is compressed; the metatarsals are translated; the phalanges are scaled about the MTP joints. The calcaneus posterior station keeps its 15.9 mm soft-tissue distance to the heel skin. The ankle pivot (`foot_*` head) does not move.
  - **Skin.** The foot is regenerated by the O2 body generator (`scripts/original_v1_foot_regen.py`). That generator reproduces the a003 foot vertex-for-vertex (2.3e-7 m), so topology, groups and weights carry over unchanged. The forefoot sections and toe split follow the same per-ray knots, anchored at the shin's front skin line. The toe length factor (0.996) is solved so that the tip lands at heel − 279.9 mm.
  - **Heel.** A smooth backward translation of 23.7 mm, full strength below 40 mm and fading to zero at 140 mm, so the lower Achilles slopes into the heel. It is solved so that the heel lands 60.0 mm behind the ankle. Nothing above 120 mm moves.
- `scripts/build_original_foot_shape_candidate_blender.py --toe-split-f 0.15814`: the C2 toe pads, against the new toe bones.

Rejected attempts, recorded so they are not repeated:
1. Remapping the existing skin coordinates directly gave a pointed heel spur and a fold at the front of the ankle.
2. Growing the generator's heel ring radially drove the heel 29 mm below the floor, and the generator then lifted the whole lower leg.

## Not resolved (do not read C3 as finished)

1. **Heel position**: 60 mm is provisional (band 50–72 mm). It needs a primary heel-to-malleolus source, or the owner's choice.
2. **Tarsal sticks are still schematic.** The navicular stick becomes 10 mm and the talus stick 30 mm. Whole-bone envelopes, centroids and contacts are still required (`canonical_tarsal_geometry_audit_v1.json`), and the tarsus stays BLOCKED for freezing.
3. **Phalanges.** These are the remainder of a surface-length partition. They are not yet matched to a stature-aware, endpoint-compatible phalanx source.
4. **Mesh**:
   - There is a small dip on the dorsum just in front of the ankle crease (medial view).
   - The upper back of the heel keeps a slight corner inherited from a003.
   - The lesser toes are still thin, and there are no nails.
5. **Rig**: still one block toe control per foot. A real toe-off needs separate MTP/IP motion.
6. **Runtime/engine**: the runtime `foot_*`/`toe_*` lengths changed. Downstream retargeting and exports (`hgpt_canonical_v4_original`) must be regenerated from C3 if it is accepted. That has not been done.
