# R53 anatomy and real-exercise evidence

Reference research only. No images, meshes, traces or third-party assets imported. The model remains independently authored. Sources describe human anatomy, not numerical skin weights or acceptance of this model.

| Primary source | Relevant evidence | Diagnostic implication and limit |
|---|---|---|
| Ludewig et al. 2009, Motion of the shoulder complex during multiplanar humeral elevation, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19181982/ ; paper https://drrobertlaprademd.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/motion-of-the-shoulder-complex-during-multiplanar-humeral-elevation-2009.pdf | Bone-pinned clavicle/scapula/humerus tracking in 12 healthy adults. Clavicle and scapula participate across elevation in multiple axes. | Inspect within-clip relative transforms, not merely equality between R41/R52. Study averages are not a mandatory motion recipe for a dumbbell press. |
| McClure et al. 2001, Direct 3-dimensional measurement of scapular kinematics during dynamic movements in vivo, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11408911/ ; university copy https://biomechanics.uoregon.edu/obl/articles/2001_mcclure_jses2001.pdf | Eight subjects; scapular bone pins. Elevation includes upward rotation, posterior tilt and external rotation. | One upward-rotation angle or fixed universal 2:1 ratio cannot validate this rig. Different methods and planes limit direct numerical comparison. |
| University of Washington deltoid atlas https://rad.washington.edu/muscle-atlas/deltoid/ | Deltoid spans clavicle/acromion/scapular spine to humeral insertion. | Investigate cap weight distribution across girdle and humerus; attachment anatomy provides no prescribed skinning percentages. |
| UAMS upper-limb muscle tables https://medicine.uams.edu/neuroscience/education/medical-school-courses/human-structure-module/anatomy-tables/muscle-tables/muscles-of-the-upper-limb/ | Pec major, latissimus and teres major have distinct origins and proximal humeral connections. | Analyze chest and posterior-fold ownership separately. A broad back slab following the arm is not justified by a lat insertion alone. |
| UAMS topographical upper limb https://medicine.uams.edu/neuroscience/education/medical-school-courses/human-structure-module/anatomy-tables/topographical-anatomy-tables/topographical-anatomy-of-the-upper-limb/ | Anterior axillary fold includes pec major; posterior fold includes latissimus and teres major. Axilla has distinct boundaries and a fat-filled interior. | A single filled crease cannot represent all these relationships. Inspect distinct folds and hollow in four views. |

## Real exercise photographs

Muscle & Fitness standing dumbbell shoulder press provides real start/overhead photographs: https://www.muscleandfitness.com/exercise/workouts/shoulder-exercises/dumbbell-shoulder-press/ . A second seated example is https://www.muscleandfitness.com/exercise/workouts/shoulder-exercises/seated-overhead-dumbbell-press/ (photography credited to Edgar Artiga). These permit qualitative phase-matched front/oblique observation. They are uncalibrated 2D examples with different body types, loading and support, not surface scans or posterior kinematics. Use no athlete silhouette as an exact numeric target and do not infer internal bone position from them. Anatomical exercise illustrations are secondary only.

The owner-approved R47 overall outline remains the project-specific shape reference. Approval of that outline does not approve folds, surface intersections, back bulging or full-motion behavior. All anatomical conclusions about the actual model require native geometry and coordinate-consistent measurements.
