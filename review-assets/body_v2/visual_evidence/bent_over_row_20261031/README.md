# Bent-over row: before vs after (31 Oct 2026)

- **Before:** the R120 surface with the r57 drives.
- **After:** candidate C1 (shoulder-extension keys, twist bones, R121 elbow and hip keys).
- **Clip:** the r57 benchmark row (hip hinge, then the pull), with a 16° arm carriage for both models. There are 49
  rendered frames.
- **Cameras:** fitted to the skeleton, fixed for the movement and identical for both models. The `back_top` view looks
  down on the shoulder blades.

**Still open on C1:** at the full pull the left posterior shoulder presses into the upper-back skin (57 pairs, against
2 on the right). The bones and key values are mirror-symmetric there, so this is a marginal contact that a small rest
asymmetry tips over. Some skin bunching is natural at the top of a row.
