# V14e finger-body refinement — experimental checkpoint

V8 remains the accepted body/knee geometry baseline. V13e remains the last hand review candidate. **V14e is an experimental, unaccepted finger-body trial**, built only from V13e. No grip refit or production change was made.

The local Blender pass splits 2,737 long interior edges across the index, middle, ring and little fingers on both hands, adding 5,474 triangles. It then relaxes the finger surface with protected push-up contact vertices and all hand patch boundaries pinned. Body count is 51,058 GLB vertices / 98,627 triangles. Maximum source-vertex displacement is 1.086 mm. Thumb, palm, wrist, non-hand body, hierarchy, exercise mechanics, equipment and animations were not intentionally changed.

Validation against frozen runtime `614033b` passes: five focused guards, bare/dressed equivalence and seven exercises sampled at 26 frames. Bone and equipment matrices are identical to V8; the posed push-up floor difference is zero. The strict 682-vertex contact guard reports zero position or skin-row change. New-vertex skin-weight loss is zero; original UV mapping agrees with V8. No degenerate triangles, nonmanifold edges or fingertip hole edges were found. Bind and posed seam bands remain unchanged from V13e, as do four edges folded above 100 degrees. The largest V13e-to-V14e posed hand shift is 0.706 mm.

Matched V13e/V14e boards are in `renders_v14e_finger_body_trial/`: open palm/back/web, closed fist palm/back/side, and curl/push-up/pull-up grips. The slightly softer finger transitions are **not a sufficient anatomical improvement**. The broad finger-body silhouette and segmented areas remain. V14e is preserved to document the result, but it is not the hand geometry baseline and should not enter grip refitting. A next candidate needs locally rebuilt shaft/joint surface geometry, including the problematic unwelded hand patches, with an explicit proof that protected floor contact remains unchanged.
