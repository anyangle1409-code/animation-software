# ORIGINAL v1 Phase 5 high-detail anatomy specification

Prepared for later execution; Phase 5 modelling has NOT started. Entry requires
stable Phase 3 foundation and Phase 4 development freeze evidence. The target is
an athletic everyday adult male suitable for exercise instruction, with convincing
silhouette and articulation. Not exaggerated bodybuilding, identity-specific
likeness, or surface noise. All forms must be independently authored from generic
anatomical knowledge and this character's own geometry and project-owned rig.

PRIMARY FORM must read at normal app distance (full body approximately 300–700
screen pixels). SECONDARY FORM must read in the standardized regional close view.
TERTIARY DETAIL is optional, subordinate to legibility and deformation. Those
pixel ranges are presentation review contexts, not runtime performance promises.
No tertiary detail is required to prove quality; do not increase complexity for
pores, veins or tiny wrinkles that disappear during exercise instruction.

## Constraints on every region

Preserve 1.82 m project-owned dimensions and frozen 63-bone rest/hierarchy.
Maintain bilateral symmetry unless an explicit authored asymmetry is documented.
Retain stable vertex IDs/topology during Phase 5 region form changes where possible;
if new topology is required, isolate it in a new candidate with explicit mapping,
counts and complete deformation/weight audits. Do not manufacture a correspondence
by nearest-vertex projection. No global remesh/subdivision applied blindly.

Use original coherent volume before secondary accents; smooth curvature must not
flatten joint landmarks. Fold-support loops must not bunch or self-intersect. Any
new local geometry must be checked in both neutral and loaded states, including
curl/overhead/grip, push-up, lunge and squat. Clothing/materials cannot conceal body
failures. Preserve every existing gate and report material regressions individually.

## Completion and review

Each regional package produces before/after fixed-camera neutral views, joint
close views and relevant loaded poses; mesh/weight audits and full comparisons
against R2, frozen development candidate and direct parent. Record owner_review
pending, commit/push real evidence and continue safe regions/diagnostics. Do not
make an irreversible subjective face/proportion choice while its parent review is
rejected or lineage is contradictory. Final anatomy approval requires owner
acceptance bound to the final candidate SHA; numerical deformation alone never
proves convincing human anatomy.
